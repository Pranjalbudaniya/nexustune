# Council Round 1: Product Strategist Proposal

## Role Mandate
Decide what is in the app and what is out. Argue for the smallest feature set that makes NexusTune better than Spotify and YouTube Music for music lovers who care about playback fidelity, fluidity, and ownership.

## Proposals

### Core v1 Scope (Must Ship)
1. **Seamless Core Playback**: YouTube Music streaming via NewPipeExtractor with high-bitrate Opus stream preference.
2. **Deep Local & Streamed Library**: Unified library merging on-device MediaStore tracks with user-saved YouTube tracks, playlists, likes, and play history.
3. **Queue Superiority**: Full queue manipulation (drag reorder, multi-select remove, swipe actions, add to next/end) that never resets or loses place.
4. **Offline Downloads**: High-speed track download to app storage with metadata embedding for fully offline listening.
5. **Fluid 120Hz M3 Expressive UI**: 4 distinctive player styles, live dynamic theming with AMOLED black, and shape morphing.

### Proposed Deferrals (v1.1)
1. **Synced Lyrics**: While desirable, relying on external lyrics scrapers introduces third-party instability during initial release. Defer to v1.1.
2. **Listening Statistics (Year in Review)**: High complexity, low immediate value for daily listening.
3. **Android Auto**: Requires specialized automotive testing and surface architecture. Defer to v1.1.

### Proposed Cuts
1. **Social Sharing Feeds / Friend Activity**: Bloat. NexusTune is personal, private, and offline-capable.
2. **Video Playback**: NexusTune is an audio streaming powerhouse, not a video player. Stream audio streams only.

## Risks & Reasoning
Commercial apps suffer from algorithmic clutter, promotional podcasts, and heavy web-view containers. By focusing purely on rapid search, rock-solid background audio, queue control, and deep visual customization, NexusTune immediately wins.
