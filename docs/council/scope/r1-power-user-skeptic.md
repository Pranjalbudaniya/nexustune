# Council Round 1: Power-User Skeptic Proposal

## Role Mandate
Assume every proposal is flawed until proven otherwise. Eliminate frivolous fluff, defend essential power-user features, and prevent regressions.

## Challenges & Core Demands

### Power-User Essentials (Non-Negotiable)
1. **Audio Equalizer & DSP**: 5-band or 10-band equalizer with bass boost and virtualizer attached to the audio session ID. Audio enthusiasts will reject an app without EQ.
2. **Gapless Playback & Crossfade**: Seamless track transitions without silence or pops.
3. **Queue Editing Integrity**: Moving 5 tracks up or down in a 200-track queue must be smooth, persistent across app kill, and not glitch currently playing audio.
4. **Backup & Restore**: Export settings, playlists, and listening history to JSON. No vendor lock-in.
5. **Local Audio Integration**: Millions of users have high-res FLAC/MP3 files on internal storage. Scan MediaStore and seamlessly integrate them into Library.

### Scope Attack
- Reject complex social feeds, algorithmic AI radio nonsense, and bloated podcast catalogs.
- Reject heavy animated background shaders that drain battery and cause thermal throttling.
